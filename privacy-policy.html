Privacy Policy

How this phone-first website handles information.

About this notice

This notice describes the website for Affordable Engine and Automotive Repair. It is not a description of every record the shop may hold offline. Contact the shop at (346) 298-9465 or 13333 Chrisman Rd, Houston, TX 77039 with privacy questions.

Information on this website

There are no contact forms, customer accounts, newsletter signups or online payments. This site does not ask you to submit personal information online. The current website code has no analytics, advertising pixels, embedded maps, videos or chat widgets, and does not sell or share visitor data for advertising.

Cookies and browser storage

The current site does not set cookies or save information in local storage, session storage or an application database in your browser. Menu and disclosure states are temporary. See our Cookie Policy for details.

Technical requests and hosting

Loading a page sends technical requests to the website host. Hosting infrastructure may receive an IP address, requested address, browser information and request time for delivery, security and reliability. This website does not operate a customer account or application database.

Calling, Maps and copying the number

A phone link opens your device’s calling application; this website does not record calls. The Maps link opens Google Maps only when you choose it; Google’s own practices apply there. Copy phone number writes only the displayed business number to your clipboard after you activate the button. It does not read your clipboard.

Your questions and offline information

For a first repair conversation, vehicle year, make, model and symptoms are usually enough. Do not send payment-card details or identification through this website. Ask the shop about any information needed for an actual work order and how its offline records are handled. To ask about access, correction or deletion of information you have given the shop, call or write to the address above. Rights and any required response process depend on applicable law; this notice does not limit them.

Changes

Adding forms, analytics, advertising, online payments or a new hosting service requires a privacy review before activation. This notice must continue to match the services actually deployed.
