Refund Policy

Information about repair payments and billing questions.

No online payments

This website does not take payments, deposits or online orders. There is no website checkout transaction to cancel or refund, and no automatic online refund process.

For work arranged with the shop

Before authorizing inspection or repairs, ask for a written work order describing the work, charges, payment terms and any applicable warranty. Ask the shop to explain cancellation, deposits, special-order parts, core charges and refund conditions that apply to your particular job before agreeing to them.

No blanket refund schedule is published

A shop-wide refund or cancellation schedule has not been provided for this preview. This page does not promise a refund period, declare deposits nonrefundable or set a deduction or restocking fee. The shop must confirm its actual terms for the job; applicable consumer rights remain in effect.

A question about a charge or repair?

Call (346) 298-9465 or visit or write to Affordable Engine and Automotive Repair, 13333 Chrisman Rd, Houston, TX 77039. Have your work order or receipt available and explain the charge or repair concern. Do not submit card numbers through this website. Discuss the circumstances and requested resolution with the shop.

Rights and written agreements

This information does not replace your work order, any applicable warranty or rights and remedies under law. It is not a no-refunds rule. Read our Terms & Conditions for the website’s limited role.
